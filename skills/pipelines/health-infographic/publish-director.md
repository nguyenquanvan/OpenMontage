# Publish Director — Health Infographic

## When to Use

Use after final review to create a publish-ready package for a health infographic video.

## Package

Include the final video, thumbnail concept, title options, description, chapter markers, subtitle file, bibliography, disclaimer, and a source-to-claim table. Keep source links direct and preserve access dates when available.

## Metadata Rules

- Titles and thumbnails may create curiosity but must not promise cures, guaranteed blood-sugar changes, or personalized outcomes.
- The description must state that the content is educational and not a substitute for professional medical advice.
- Disclose uncertainty and important population limits that materially affect interpretation.
- Do not claim endorsement by cited institutions or researchers.

## Quality Checklist

- Human approval is required before release.
- Video, subtitles, citations, chapters, and disclaimer are included.
- Metadata matches the actual evidence and does not exaggerate.
- Rights/provenance records are retained with the export bundle.

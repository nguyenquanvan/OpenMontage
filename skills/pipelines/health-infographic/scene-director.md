# Scene Director — Health Infographic

## When to Use

Use to translate the approved health script into an editorial infographic scene plan.

## Visual Grammar

Alternate among ingredient/product photography, simplified anatomy, molecule or process diagrams, comparison cards, timelines, evidence ladders, stat cards, myth/reality cards, and recap screens. Do not use three consecutive scenes of the same type. Every scene needs a learning objective, narration range, visual intent, required assets, overlay text, `source_ref`, and duration.

Use generated imagery only for text-free illustration or photography. Render all labels, numbers, citations, arrows, legends, and charts in the approved composition runtime. Keep sensitive anatomy non-graphic and use abstract diagrams when realism would distract or alarm.

## Professional Motion Pack

Use these deterministic Remotion `cut.type` values where they improve understanding:

- `ingredient_spotlight`: ingredient or product hero with facts and slow parallax.
- `mechanism_flow`: 2–5 causal or process nodes; mark only the key active node with `emphasis`.
- `evidence_ladder`: 2–5 evidence layers with `strength` and `status`; do not convert uncertainty into false precision.
- `myth_reality`: one misconception, one correction, and an optional careful takeaway.
- `timeline_steps`: 2–6 ordered stages with optional time labels.

Plan motion rhythm across the full video: about 20% strong motion for hero moments, 60% medium explanatory motion, and 20% quiet reading/rest scenes. Select no more than three transition families from `fade`, `slide-left`, `slide-right`, `slide-up`, `wipe-left`, `iris`, `zoom-through`, and `light-sweep`. Reserve `zoom-through`, `iris`, and `light-sweep` for section peaks.

## Reference Handling

Translate reference observations into general properties such as warm cream backgrounds, restrained motion, and image-text layouts. Do not recreate distinctive frames, thumbnails, wording, or shot order.

## Quality Checklist

- Timeline covers the script with no gaps.
- Visual type changes support comprehension, not novelty.
- Every chart or mechanism can be produced by available tools.
- Citation placement and reading time are defined.
- Disclaimer screen is included and legible.
